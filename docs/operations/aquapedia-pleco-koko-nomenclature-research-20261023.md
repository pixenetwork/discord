# *Pseudoqolus koko* nomenclature and uncertain mitochondrial history (2026-10-23)

## Taxonomic evidence

**Original description:** Fisch-Muller, Montoya-Burgos, Le Bail & Covain (2012), *Diversity of the Ancistrini (Siluriformes: Loricariidae) from the Guianas*, *Cybium* 36(1), describes `Panaqolus koko` from the Maroni/Marouini drainage of French Guiana, including examined museum vouchers. [Published original text](https://www.sfi-cybium.fr/sites/default/files/pdfs-cybium/11-FischMuller%5BGuianas%5D%20colour.pdf).

**Later generic revision:** Lujan, Cramer, Covain, Fisch-Muller & López-Fernández (2017), *Multilocus molecular phylogeny of ornamental wood-eating catfishes*, *Molecular Phylogenetics and Evolution* 109:321–336, [DOI 10.1016/j.ympev.2016.12.040](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ympev.2016.12.040), establishes `Pseudoqolus` with `Panaqolus koko` as its type species. The accepted combination is `Pseudoqolus koko (Fisch-Muller & Covain, 2012)`, as also indexed by the [IUCN Red List](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2022-1.RLTS.T176217882A176217903.en), [FishBase summary](https://www.fishbase.se/summary/66596), and Eschmeyer's [species record 72287](https://researcharchive.calacademy.org/research/ichthyology/catalog/fishcatget.asp?spid=72287).

Do not confuse older registry search results using `Panaqolus koko` with proof the 2017 reassignment was invalid. The contemporary FishBase summary itself labels `Pseudoqolus koko` and its separate synonym page marks original `Panaqolus koko` as the older combination.

## Biological and gene-tree uncertainty

The 2017 paper tests a **tentative historical mitochondrial introgression hypothesis** involving a co-occurring `Peckoltia` species, *P. otali*. It did not include `P. otali` in that phylogenetic analysis and did not prove the historical hybridization event. Its mitochondrial and nuclear gene trees provide different evidence; their disagreement should be recorded as an **unconfirmed hypothesis**, if included in Aquapedia's research gene tree, with a dashed uncertainty marker and clear source citation. No confirmed parent strains, breeding crosses, breeder lineage, inheritance pattern or current aquarium animal identification follows from this evolutionary research. Further, the paper evaluated gut contents of only one *koko* specimen; do not generalize a definitive domestic feeding protocol from it.
