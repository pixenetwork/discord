## Hypancistrus vandragti — original name and later combination

- **Original publication:** Lujan and Armbruster (2011), *Two New Genera and Species of Ancistrini (Siluriformes: Loricariidae) from the Western Guiana Shield*, *Copeia* 2011(2):216–225, DOI [10.1643/CI-10-008](https://doi.org/10.1643/CI-10-008). The description originally named **Micracanthicus vandragti**.
- **Subsequent phylogenetic reassignment:** Lujan and collaborators (2017) treated `Micracanthicus` as a synonym within `Hypancistrus`; the peer-reviewed 2025 [Neotropical Ichthyology discussion](https://www.ni.bio.br/1982-0224-2024-0080/) explicitly recounts the 2011 original and 2017 synonymy. [GBIF Catalogue of Life taxon](https://www.gbif.org/taxon/Q3KHM) currently lists **Hypancistrus vandragti (Lujan & Armbruster, 2011)**, original combination `Micracanthicus vandragti`.
- **Publication limitation:** This establishes a taxonomic naming history, **not** genetic breeding ancestry. A trade specimen labelled with either name is not authenticated by this literature alone.

## Panaqolus changae — original 2002 species paper

- **Original species description:** Chockley and Armbruster (2002), *Panaque changae, a new species of catfish (Siluriformes: Loricariidae) from eastern Peru*, *Ichthyological Exploration of Freshwaters* **13(1):81–90**, [author-hosted original paper](https://webhome.auburn.edu/~armbrjw/Panaque_changae.pdf). Original published combination: **Panaque changae**.
- [GBIF Catalogue of Life](https://www.gbif.org/taxon/4C9CL) recognizes **Panaqolus changae (Chockley & Armbruster, 2002)** and identifies `Panaque changae` as its original combination. This naming history does not prove any aquarium fish's identification.
- **Incorrect-as-original citation:** DOI [10.1590/S1679-62252014000100006](https://doi.org/10.1590/S1679-62252014000100006) is a *Panaqolus purusiensis* redescription/identification key. It may be comparative background but must **not** substitute for the 2002 original description.
- The original author-hosted 2002 paper is **already cited in the existing Discord starter**, but the public Aquapedia draft URL has not previously been linked; any new message is therefore limited to a back-reference plus the naming distinction, not another copy of the paper.
- Existing public draft: https://aquapedia.app/plecopedia/research/pana-changae/
- Existing bot-owned thread: `1561174473669873706`.
